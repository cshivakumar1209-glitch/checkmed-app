The 3 players that control your drug prices

PBM — Pharmacy Benefit Manager
The middlemen
PBMs sit between insurance companies and pharmacies. They negotiate rebates from drug manufacturers and decide which drugs are covered — but often keep a portion of those savings.
  • CVS Caremark
  • Express Scripts (Cigna)
  • OptumRx (UnitedHealth)
  • GoodRx (discount card)
Insurance Companies
The plan makers
Your health insurer determines which drugs are "covered" and at what copay level. They organize medications into formulary tiers — the higher the tier, the more you pay.
  • Blue Cross Blue Shield
  • UnitedHealthcare
  • Aetna / Cigna / Humana
  • Medicare Part D plans
Drug Manufacturers
The price setters
Pharmaceutical companies set the original list price of drugs. Many offer Patient Assistance Programs that provide free or heavily discounted medications based on income.
  • Novo Nordisk (Ozempic)
  • AbbVie (Humira, Skyrizi)
  • Pfizer (Eliquis, Xeljanz)
  • 200+ programs tracked

About CheckMed

Helping patients navigate the confusing world of drug pricing — free, always.

🎯
Our mission
CheckMed was built to give patients the same pricing information that insurers, PBMs, and pharmacies already have — so you can make smarter decisions and pay less at the pharmacy.
🔒
Privacy first
We don't require an account or store your searches. CheckMed is not affiliated with any insurer, PBM, or drug manufacturer. We have no financial incentive to promote any specific option.
📊
Data sources
Pricing data is sourced from publicly available formulary files, CMS datasets, manufacturer websites, and GoodRx pricing APIs. Prices are updated daily and shown as estimates.
🤖
AI-powered suggestions
Our AI suggestion engine uses Claude (by Anthropic) to analyze prices and suggest cheaper alternatives, generic substitutes, and assistance programs specific to your medication.

How it works

1
Search your medication
Type the name of your prescription drug. We cover over 2,400 medications — from common generics like Metformin to specialty biologics like Humira and Ozempic. Search by brand or generic name.
